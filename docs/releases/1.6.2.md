# DEGIRO Importer 1.6.2

## Fixes

- Import currency conversions as linked internal cash-transfer pairs, including
  order-linked partial fills. Incomplete or ambiguous conversions block review.
- Keep transaction fees in their source currency: EUR fees on USD trades become
  separate EUR charges. Cash reconciliation now includes merged trade fees.
- Preserve cash debits for dividend corrections and negative interest. Import
  tax/fee refunds, bond coupons, promotional credits, and monetary money-market
  fund movements that were previously skipped.
- Continue excluding internal trading/Flatex sweeps to avoid double-counting cash.
- Prevent saving a reviewed FX leg if its companion fails validation. Partial
  persistence is reported accurately, and retries save only missing activities.
- Record the actual release version in importer provenance metadata.

## Validation

- 229 synthetic unit tests and both packaged-addon browser smoke tests passed.
- A private full-history statement accounted for every source row, imported
  without rejected activities into disposable Wealthfolio 3.6.1, and created
  no activities on repeat import.
- Native cash holdings matched source economics at cent precision; consolidated
  EUR matched the latest Flatex bank balance. Source foreign-currency rounding
  differences are preserved, not filled with invented balancing transactions.
- Private statements and test databases are not included in this release.

## Upgrade notes

- These fixes do not rewrite activities imported by older versions. Validate
  the complete history in a fresh account; overlapping imports do not repair
  previous fee currencies, missing FX legs, or incorrectly signed income.
- Wealthfolio 3.6.1 normalizes amounts to positive values. Dividend reversals
  are recorded as cash charges, preserving cash but not correcting dividend
  income reporting.
- Native cash validation used manual security identities to isolate cash
  handling; live market-data mappings and valuations still require review.

## Compatibility

- Wealthfolio: 3.6.1
- Add-on SDK: 3.6.1
